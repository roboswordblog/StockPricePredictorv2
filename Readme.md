# Stock Price Predictor V2

This is the second version of  my stock price Predictor V1.
I just used one indicator to decide whether to buy the stock or not. 
This time, I will be using 3 to figure out whether to buy the stock or not.

## The Indicators
The indicators for this will be:
  1. Past 10 Days(Expansion of Stock Price Predictor V1)
  2. Todays stock price in realitivity to last years lowest stock price and this years lowest
  3. Text Transformer of whether the Stocks will go up or down based on news headlines.
